The problem

The oldest savings tool in the world still runs on trust and a notebook.

A group pays a fixed amount every round. One person takes the whole pot each time, until everyone has had a turn. No interest and no bank, just a lump sum sooner than saving alone, kept honest by social pressure. It has a different name almost everywhere:

Nigeria
Ajo / Esusu
Ghana and Caribbean
Susu
Mexico and Latin America
Tanda
Kenya
Chama
India
Chit fund
China
Hui

The mechanism works. The trust does not scale. One member disappears with the pot, or just stops paying, and the group has no recourse and no ledger to prove what happened.

The idea

A circle you can trust because the chain enforces it.

  1. 1Lock a bond to join a circle.
  2. 2Pay your share each round, by hand or by autopay.
  3. 3Miss one and your bond covers your share. The pot still pays out on time.
  4. 4Your on-time record follows your wallet into the next circle you join.

No admin keys. No custodian. The program is the treasurer.

One round

Six members pay in. One takes the pot.

This round's recipient Bond locked, waiting its turn Vault owned by the program

Order is join order, or drawn on-chain from the network's own randomness when the circle fills. The seed is stored, so the app recomputes the draw and checks it.

Reputation

A reliability record that is not stuck inside one app.

Every wallet carries one Kin Score on-chain: on-time, late and missed payments, streak, circles completed. It updates as circles run and follows the wallet everywhere.

Anyone can paste a wallet address into Kin and read its score before inviting it. A circle can also turn away any wallet with a missed payment on record.

Solana native

Every feature needed the phone and the chain together.

MWA 2.0.3
Every signature happens in the wallet. Kin builds the transaction and never touches a key.
Seed Vault
On a Seeker that signature is biometric-backed key custody, the standard Mobile Wallet Adapter path, used on every action.
Genesis Token
A circle can require a Seeker Genesis Token. The program itself checks the Token-2022 account and its mint authority.
SKR track
A circle can be denominated in tSKR instead of tUSDC, contesting the separate $10k SKR integration prize with a real circle, not a mention on a slide.
Autopay
A capped, revocable SPL delegation. Anyone can trigger a collection, but it moves one contribution into that circle's own vault and nothing else.
Verifiable order
Random order comes from the slot hash when the circle fills. The seed is stored and anyone can recompute it.
Test faucet
A separate devnet program owns both test tokens' mint authority, so anyone can get funds in one tap with no server and no secret key.
Home widget
A phone-only surface: the circle that needs attention most, glanceable without opening the app, the answer to a savings app's weakest habit loop.
.skr names
Wherever Kin shows a wallet, it checks mainnet for a .skr or .sol name, the kind a Seeker hands out by default, and shows that instead of an address.

Good to know

Wallets often cannot simulate devnet transactions, so Phantom may warn before you confirm. The same flow simulates normally on mainnet.

Phantom may also show its own identity notice. Google's link checker confirms Kin's signing keys, and the README explains both.

Judging criteria

Built against all four.

25%, stickiness and product fit

A habit, not a mint

A recurring financial ritual people already run, with a home-screen widget, reminders, streaks and a score worth protecting.

25%, user experience

One tap, one signature

Pay, collect for the group or claim a payout, each in the wallet on the phone. A clear landing page, a no-wallet simulation and free test funds.

25%, innovation

Enforced, not promised

Bonds, portable reputation and verifiable randomness live in the program, not in a claim the app makes.

25%, presentation

Checkable, not just clickable

94 automated tests across program, logic and client, and an in-app Proof screen anyone can run.

Solo build, paired with AI development, which the hackathon rules welcome.
